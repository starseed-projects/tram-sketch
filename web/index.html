    Stops

      How to

      • Select a line, then click the map to add stops to its end.
      • Missed a stop? Click on or near the selected line's track to insert a stop right there.
      • Stops snap onto the nearest main road (level boarding on the line).
      • Click an existing stop to add it to the selected line (interchange).
      • Drag stops to move them; track re-routes automatically.
      • Ctrl-click the selected line's track to add a bend point; drag it to steer the route. Right-click a bend point to remove it.
      • Right-click a stop to rename or remove it.
      • Drag the round handles on curved stretches to shape the curve; right-click a handle to reset it.
      • Right-click the track to make a stretch a transit mall (street closed to cars), an own corridor (e.g. through a park), bridge or tunnel — these are smooth curves between bend points.
      • Set a line's type to Cable car for straight spans between stations; bend points act as towers.
      • Zoom in to street level to see the double track alignment.
      • Esc deselects · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo
